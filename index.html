Frequently Asked Questions

Is the KUCCPS application submitted through the official portal?

Yes. All KUCCPS applications are submitted directly through the official KUCCPS portal at students.kuccps.net. We guide you through the process and submit on your behalf using the official system.

How do I know this is not a scam?

We are a verified student service based at Chuka University. We provide proof of every submission, use official government portals only, and never ask for your M-Pesa PIN or password. We also offer a clear refund policy.

Do you ask for my M-Pesa PIN?

Never. We will NEVER ask for your M-Pesa PIN, ATM PIN, or any password. Payment is made via standard M-Pesa transfers only. If anyone asks for your PIN claiming to be us, report them immediately.

What happens if the portal rejects my application?

If an application is rejected due to a portal or system issue, we will resubmit for free. If the rejection is due to ineligibility (e.g., missing grades), we will notify you immediately and discuss your options, including a partial refund.

How long does it take to process my application?

Most applications are processed within 24–48 hours. You'll receive an initial response within 30 minutes of submitting your details, and we'll keep you updated via WhatsApp throughout the process.
